# What changed on your preview — and how to check each of your 52 points

*Release 21, checked on your preview on 28.10.2026. At the very bottom of every page, shop and admin, the line „Testumgebung · Release 21 vom …“ tells you
that you are looking at this version.*

## What is new since release 20, in your terms

- **The rim and tyre setup follows your documents to the letter.** Where a Gutachten lists the tyre combinations for
  different sizes front and rear — your ABE 55255 does, in V19 — the shop offers exactly those pairs as a Komplettrad,
  two wheels per axle, and shows each axle's Auflagen under „Vorderachse“ and „Hinterachse“. A size the document allows
  on the rear axle only is never offered in front.
- **One maker, one profile per axle.** Where the document says „Es sind nur Reifen eines Herstellers und achsweise eines
  Profiltyps zulässig“, the tyres offered follow it, and the sentence is shown where it limits the choice.
- **Every approved width and offset can be chosen.** A wheel approved for a car in two sizes of one diameter — say
  8,5J and 9,5J at 19 inch — now offers both, each with its own answer.
- **„Fahrzeugschein scannen“ works on the phone.** Take the photo, check the two numbers, press „Übernehmen“: HSN and
  TSN are filled in. Before this release the phone dropped the photo.
- **The admin's lists sort and search the way people type.** Reifen, Fahrzeuge and the Protokoll sort by their columns,
  as Felgen and Bestellungen do. The searches find „TÜV“ when you type „TUEV“, a Nachtrag by the ABE number it belongs
  to, a car by „5 Z90“ as well as „0005 Z90“, and a wheel when two spaces slipped in.
- **On the phone, the admin names front and rear** in the tyre combinations, and an Auflage's level is readable in full.
- **Smaller things you may notice:** the number of wheels found reads „2 Felgen“ (it read „2Felgen“), the hero says
  under „Fahrzeug wählen“ why it is waiting, and 40 points from our own review of the admin since release 20.

## Open decisions for the meeting

The rim and tyre logic, with six worked examples from your Borbet documents, is in a separate one-page sheet for the
meeting. The decisions only you can make:

1. **The table of tyre load at high speed (your admin point 15).** Your ABE 55255 prints one; with it, an E46 at
   226 km/h gets its tyres confirmed. Your Nachtrag prints none; without it, an E90 at 222 km/h cannot. We recommend
   keeping the table where the document prints it, and deciding together how documents like the Nachtrag, which refer
   to the vehicle papers, should be handled.
2. **The invoice (your admin point 17).** Who issues it, and does the shop keep a copy?
3. **How many wheels can be ordered (your point 17).** Today 1 to 8, four chosen first. Should 1–3 be possible?
4. **The tyres' EU label.** Who supplies the label data — the supplier's file, or entry by hand?
5. **Reviews (your point 19).** You asked for stars entered in the admin; the shop shows verified buyers' reviews only,
   because invented ratings are not allowed (§ 5 UWG). We can import real reviews from Google or your old shop with
   their source named.
6. **The „Nur die Felgen bestellen“ button (your point 25).** It was removed as you asked; „Nur Felgen“ or „Komplettrad“
   is now chosen beside the price. Is that what you want?
7. **Customer accounts.** Your first plan left them out; the shop has them (the order link and the order history use
   them). Keep them?
8. **Still waiting for your answer:** which valves without sensors to offer (point 15); whether your Gutachten names
   three or four combinations (admin point 8); your demo vehicle file (admin point 2); Motec photos, if you want them
   in the turning wheel (point 9); the end of your sentence „Product Detail Page needs …“ (point 34).
