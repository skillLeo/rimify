# Human QA — the ledger

The human QA pass of 28.10.2026 (`HUMAN-QA-FULL.md`, brief §9): every finding, as a person found it, with what they
expected (and where that expectation comes from in `EXPECTATIONS.md`), what they got, its class and severity, and the
commit that fixed it. Pictures are evidence and stay out of the repository while it is public: they are under
`storage/qa-evidence/` on the machine that ran the pass, named in the „Bild“ column.

**Class** — Bug: wrong result, error, data lost, crash · Friction: it works, a person struggles · Rule: deliberate,
from regulation or an R-rule — the fix is a German explanation on screen · Expectation gap: does what we decided, not
what he expects — not changed, it goes to the meeting.
**Severity** — S1: a verdict, a price or data wrong, a crash, a security gap, or a client item marked Done that fails ·
S2: a task cannot be completed · S3: friction · S4: cosmetic.

**Personas** — O: the shop owner, checking · S: staff operator · P: customer on the phone · D: customer on the desktop ·
R: returning customer.
